# Pesapad

**Simple customer and supplier bill tracking for small businesses.**

Pesapad helps business owners keep track of customer balances, supplier bills, payment history, and follow-ups from an Android app.

## Features

- **Customer bills:** Record and review customer balances.
- **Supplier bills:** Keep track of supplier bills and amounts payable.
- **Bill details:** Store transaction information and notes where supported.
- **Payment insights:** Review payment history and customer or supplier insights.
- **Follow-up actions:** Access available contact and reminder/promise features.
